# Privacy Policy — Biostatistics Mastery

_Last updated: [DATE]_

**Biostatistics Mastery** ("the app") is an offline educational app for Zoology and Life Sciences students, published by **[PUBLISHER NAME / INSTITUTION]**.

## Summary
The app does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network access (it does not request the Internet permission).

## What stays on your device
- **Learning progress, bookmarks, notes and settings** you create are stored locally on your device only.
- If you choose **Export progress**, the app writes a JSON file to a location you pick with the system file picker. If you choose **Import progress**, it reads a file you pick. The app never sends these files anywhere.
- **Share** and **Print** use Android's standard system dialogs; the content you share or print is chosen by you.

## Permissions
The app requests no Android permissions.

## Children
The app is intended for university-level students and is not directed at children under 13.

## Data deletion
Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all progress and notes held by the app. Files you exported yourself remain where you saved them until you delete them.

## Educational disclaimer
Content and calculators are for learning. They are not intended for clinical, diagnostic or regulatory decisions.
